## This week: auto-drafted replies to common enquiries

Most small businesses answer the same handful of questions again and again: "How much for…?", "Are you free on…?", "Do you cover Gateshead?" Answering them takes minutes each, often in the evening.

### What you'll set up
A short "answer sheet" plus an AI assistant that drafts replies for you to check and send. You stay in control: nothing goes out without you pressing send.

### Steps (about an hour)
1. **List your top 10 questions.** Look through last month's emails or messages and note what people ask most.
2. **Write your answer sheet.** For each question, write the facts: prices or price ranges, areas covered, opening hours, lead times, what you don't do.
3. **Save it as a document** you can paste from (Google Docs, Word or Notes).
4. **Use an AI assistant to draft.** When an enquiry arrives, paste the answer sheet and the customer's message into an AI chat tool and ask: *"Using only the facts in my answer sheet, draft a short, friendly reply. If the answer isn't in the sheet, say I'll confirm."*
5. **Check and send.** Fix anything wrong and update the sheet so next time is better.

### Things to watch
- Don't paste sensitive customer details (bank details, health info) into AI tools.
- Always read the draft before sending; AI can get details wrong.
- Keep prices in the sheet up to date.
